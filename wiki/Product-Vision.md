# Product Vision

Margin helps a person understand their money before it disappears into unexamined spending.

The app combines salary, expenses, planned savings, and recurring commitments into a calm view of what is available now and what will remain after important obligations.

## Experience principles

- Add common information quickly.
- Make calculations visible and explainable.
- Prefer useful summaries over excessive analytics.
- Keep the interface minimalist, dark, and readable.
- Keep real data local.
